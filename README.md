# ⛏️ The Baseline Dig – Backtrace

### Dig deeper. Find the gap. Build understanding.

**Baseline Dig** is an AI-powered learning diagnosis project based on the concept of **Prerequisite Archaeology**.

Backtrace aims to identify the hidden knowledge gaps that prevent learners from understanding a particular topic. Instead of teaching everything from scratch, it traces misunderstandings back to their foundational concepts and focuses on what the learner actually needs to improve.

---

## 💡 The Problem

Many learners struggle with advanced topics not because the topic itself is too difficult, but because they have gaps in the foundational concepts required to understand it.

Traditional learning platforms often provide the same explanations and lessons to everyone without identifying exactly where their understanding breaks.

## 🚀 Our Solution

Backtrace approaches learning differently.

It works backwards through prerequisite concepts to discover the root of a learner's misunderstanding.

### How It Works

1. **Enter a Topic:** The learner provides any topic they want to understand.
2. **Concept Mapping:** The system breaks the topic into smaller prerequisite concepts.
3. **Diagnostic Assessment:** The learner answers questions to demonstrate their understanding.
4. **Gap Detection:** The system analyses incorrect answers and identifies possible misconceptions.
5. **Personalized Learning:** The learner receives targeted explanations for concepts they haven't understood.
6. **Reassessment:** The learner can check whether their understanding has improved.
